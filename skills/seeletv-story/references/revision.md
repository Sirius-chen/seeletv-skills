# Focused Revision

## Establish the revision contract

Identify the requested outcome, what must remain, and whether the user wants diagnosis, a proposed approach, or revised text. Honor instructions such as keeping every line, preserving the ending, or changing dialogue only. If the requested improvement cannot fit those constraints, explain the specific conflict and offer the smallest viable choice.

Read the actual supplied version. Separate a source inconsistency from a deliberate mystery, ellipsis, unreliable viewpoint, or stylistic choice. Mark missing source coverage rather than filling it with invented certainty.

## Diagnose with evidence

Check only the dimensions relevant to the request:

- Is the audience's intended experience understandable, and does the ending follow from what precedes it?
- Do important actions have credible motives and consequences? Does a resolution rely on unavailable information, unexplained power, or coincidence?
- Are relationships, who knows what, prop ownership, and world rules internally consistent?
- Do scenes repeat information without changing its meaning or use? Is important action replaced by explanation?
- Can the dialogue belong to these people, and does it fit the available time?

For a substantive issue, point to the source moment, describe its effect, and suggest a concrete remedy. Do not manufacture numerical quality scores or require a universal beat sheet, fixed act percentages, a twist, or a happy ending.

## Revise in order of impact

Address the agreed causal or character problem before polishing sentences that depend on it. Prefer a targeted change that preserves the user's distinctive choices. For a dialogue-only request, do not introduce a new character, prop, location, action, or revelation to solve the problem unless the user expands the scope.

Produce one coherent revised version from current decisions. Do not stack amendments onto an obsolete draft or retain deleted ideas in explanatory negatives. Keep useful ambiguity and the user's chosen emotional register.

## Verify and deliver

Read the revision against the locked facts and requested scope. Check that any changed setup, consequence, name, knowledge state, or object transfer remains coherent elsewhere in the supplied material. For a local rewrite, report any necessary change outside the permitted excerpt rather than silently performing it.

Deliver revised text when asked to rewrite, with a concise account of material changes and any unresolved constraint. For critique-only work, stop at findings and proposed fixes. Do not start asset or video generation as a consequence of a writing task.
